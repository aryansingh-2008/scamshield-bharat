Fraudulent Telegram/WhatsApp VIP channels promise 300% monthly returns using fake Demat profit screenshots to funnel victims into unregulated mule accounts.

2. Regulatory & Authority Impersonation

Forged SEBI certificates, fake RBI letters, and police notices threaten victims with tax penalties, narcotics packages, and coercive 'Digital Arrest'.

3. Fake KYC Expiry & Account Block Panics

Coercive SMS alerts claim 'Trading Demat Account blocked tonight due to incomplete KYC' with clone phishing links (bit.ly / .xyz) capturing OTPs.

4. Malicious APK & Screen Sharing Traps

Victims are instructed to sideload customized APK files or screen-sharing tools (AnyDesk, QuickSupport) to drain bank accounts.
